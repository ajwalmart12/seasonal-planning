Decision
When and how much inventory to commit given uncertain future demand?
Recommendation
Stage commitments in three tranches aligned to signal confidence; preserve flexibility early, commit core capacity at moderate confidence, release remaining at high confidence.
Evidence
Base case probability rising as leading indicators converge · Staged commitment reduces both excess-inventory and availability risk · Historical patterns suggest multi-wave demand shape
Primary Risk
Probability estimates are judgmental and may not capture structural demand shifts or supply disruptions outside the model's training window.

Assumptions & Limitations
  • All scenario data is synthetically generated; no employer-specific event data is used.

  • Demand shape profiles are constructed from publicly available seasonal patterns.
  • Commitment costs and flexibility trade-offs are simplified for demonstration.
